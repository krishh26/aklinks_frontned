Content & Traffic Policy

Clear guidelines to ensure safe, high-quality content and trustworthy traffic across AKLinks.

Last updated: February 8, 2026

This policy sets the standards for content shared through AKLinks and the traffic sources that access those links.

We require original, lawful, and user-focused experiences. These rules protect users, advertisers, and publishers and help keep the platform compliant with ad network requirements.

1

Purpose

This policy applies to all users, links, and traffic sources that interact with AKLinks.

2

Allowed Content

We support original, useful, and lawful content that provides value to visitors. Examples include:

  • •Educational articles, tutorials, and resources.
  • •Original media, blogs, product pages, or service pages.
  • •Community content that follows platform rules and local laws.
3

Prohibited Content

Links that include or promote the following are not allowed:

  • •Illegal products, services, or activities.
  • •Violence, hate, harassment, or discriminatory content.
  • •Adult or sexually explicit content.
  • •Malware, phishing, or deceptive downloads.
  • •Copyright infringement or counterfeit products.
  • •Content that misleads users about the destination or intent.
4

Traffic Quality Standards

Only genuine, user-initiated traffic is allowed. The following practices are prohibited:

  • •Bot traffic, automated clicks, or click farms.
  • •Incentivized clicks or forced redirects.
  • •Traffic masking, VPN manipulation, or proxy abuse.
  • •Misleading popups, fake download buttons, or auto-redirect chains.
5

User Experience Requirements

We require a clean and transparent user experience:

  • •No misleading claims or false scarcity tactics.
  • •Clear navigation and easy access to the destination content.
  • •Respect for user privacy and consent requirements.
6

Destination Quality

Destination pages should contain meaningful, original content. Thin pages, parked domains, or pages with little value may be restricted from monetization.

7

Compliance With Advertising Policies

AKLinks follows the requirements of major advertising providers. Users must comply with:

  • •Google Publisher Policies
  • •Google Publisher Restrictions
  • •Google Search Content Quality Guidelines
8

Enforcement

We may remove links, limit monetization, or suspend accounts that violate this policy. Enforcement decisions are made to protect users, advertisers, and the integrity of the platform.

9

Reporting Abuse

If you encounter harmful content or suspicious traffic sources, please report them via the Contact Us page. We investigate all reports promptly.
